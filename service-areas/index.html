    Why Where It Happened Matters

    New York law does not change between Huntington and Brooklyn. Almost everything else does, and the practical differences decide more claims than most people expect.

    Who Took the Report

    County police, a village department, the State Police on the parkways, or the NYPD. Which one responded determines where the report comes from, and requesting it from the wrong place costs weeks.

    How Short the Deadline Is

    Most New York injury claims run three years. A claim involving a city, a town or a public authority — a bus, a municipal vehicle, the road itself — begins with a notice of claim on a deadline measured in weeks.

    What Evidence Exists

    A dense commercial street is overlooked by cameras and full of witnesses. An unlit county road at night is neither. What can be recovered, and how quickly it disappears, varies enormously by where you were.

    Where It Would Be Heard

    Riverhead for Suffolk, Mineola for Nassau, Jamaica for Queens, Downtown Brooklyn for Kings. Most matters settle without anyone appearing, but the venue is a real consideration in how a case is run.
