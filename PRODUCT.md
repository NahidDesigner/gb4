# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary visitor is a person injured in a motor-vehicle collision or premises incident on Long Island or in New York City. They may be on a phone shortly after an accident, under financial and medical stress, and trying to decide whether to call a lawyer. Family members and previous-client referrals are important secondary visitors.

## Product Purpose

GB Law Firm's site explains the firm's personal-injury services, establishes trust, and helps a qualified visitor request a free, confidential case review or call (516) 444-1000. Success means the visitor quickly understands where the firm works, what it handles, why it is credible, and what to do next.

## Positioning

GB Law Firm is the institutional identity formed by the joining of Phil NY Law and Buznik Law. It positions itself as a trial-ready Long Island personal-injury firm with local familiarity across Suffolk and Nassau counties, rather than as two individual-attorney brands.

## Operating Context

The experience must work especially well on a phone, in a high-stress and time-sensitive situation. Visitors need rapid orientation, legible explanations, credible proof, obvious phone access, and a clear path to a free evaluation. Local places, roads, courts, and service coverage are meaningful factual context.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static custom HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with no framework, page builder, or build step.
- The current work is homepage-only.
- The existing hero's layout, image, copy, heading, and typography remain untouched.
- Client-approved body copy remains verbatim. Sections may be cut or merged when approved, but no new content section may be invented.
- The existing footer and mobile sticky call bar remain.
- The homepage preloader does not appear on other pages.
- No attorney portraits appear on the homepage.
- Existing SEO metadata, structured data, analytics, forms, handlers, and site behavior must be preserved.
- Motion must remain progressively enhanced, respect reduced-motion preferences, and leave the page usable if JavaScript fails.
- The current visual rules outside the locked elements are open to reconsideration. The logo's navy and gold are brand assets; page-wide palette usage and fonts outside the locked hero are not yet confirmed as client mandates.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: GB Law Firm.
- The GB Law Firm logo is a confirmed brand asset.
- The homepage presents the firm as an institution, not as a pair of attorney personalities.
- The locked hero and its headline are preserved exactly.
- The existing footer and mobile sticky call bar are preserved.
- The voice is direct, credible, calm, and specific. It must not make unsupported claims or turn a distressed visitor's situation into spectacle.

## Evidence on Hand

- Logo and hero assets in `assets/`.
- Google review source data in `GUREVICH_LAW_Google_Reviews.csv`; reviews must retain their original attribution to Phil Gurevich or Gurevich Law where applicable.
- Professional and rating badges in `assets/badges/`.
- Existing practice-area, service-area, testimonials, case-results, and attorney pages.
- Verified location and contact facts: 580 Oak St, Copiague, NY 11726; (516) 444-1000; service across Long Island and New York City.
- Published coverage includes motor-vehicle collisions, premises liability, wrongful death, New York no-fault and serious-injury threshold claims, and uninsured/underinsured motorist claims.
- Settlement figures currently present in redesign materials are placeholders pending client verification and must not be presented as verified evidence.
- Several inner-page legal drafts remain unverified and must not be promoted as settled firm positions or legal advice.

## Product Principles

1. Orient a stressed visitor before asking for attention.
2. Earn trust through real evidence and local specificity, not generic prestige styling.
3. Make the next action obvious without overwhelming the page with repeated calls to action.
4. Present one coherent firm while preserving accurate attribution and legal caveats.
5. Never fabricate outcomes, legal claims, testimonials, or geographic coverage.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The homepage must support keyboard navigation, visible focus, readable contrast, reduced motion, practical touch targets, and responsive layouts from small phones through desktop. Core content and contact actions must remain available when JavaScript is unavailable.
